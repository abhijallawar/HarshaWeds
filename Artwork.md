# Artwork

The temple, floral frame, and mountains were generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool, then compressed to WebP for use as separate layers. The foliage, sprig, lotus, and event motifs are original SVGs in this project.

## Temple — public/art/temple.webp

> Original illustrated foreground asset for an elegant Indian wedding invitation website, isolated on genuinely transparent background. Hand-painted heritage Indian miniature gouache with finely engraved details and soft watercolor paper texture. One full, richly detailed golden sandstone Indian temple complex, symmetrical front elevation, layered carved shikhara towers with one tallest at center, a wide pillared mandapa, long marble steps descending toward the viewer, small glowing brass diyas symmetrically on the lower steps. Keep the silhouette well-defined, little sprigs around the base only, all edges fully contained in a wide landscape composition. Stone in honey, rose sandstone and muted warm gold with warm cream highlights. Perspective straight-on and from the base of the stairs, richly ceremonial and architecturally intricate. Transparent alpha all around, including sky and sides; no mountains, no background, no people, no text, no watermark. Main tower centered, substantial temple width, no foliage taller than the stairs.

## Floral frame — public/art/floral-frame.webp

> Original transparent isolated edge decorations for an Indian wedding invitation website. Wide landscape ratio 3:2, detailed hand-painted vintage Indian miniature gouache and fine engraving, elegant and botanically abundant. A thin elegant arch of deep olive leaves with a few burgundy roses, peach and cream blossoms, strings of marigolds and two antique hanging brass bells at both upper corners. At each bottom corner a small elegant teal-and-indigo Indian peacock surrounded by dark olive plants and burgundy roses facing inward. Design composition as a frame: outer edges carry artwork, center 65% is entirely genuinely TRANSPARENT, and sides have no posts, only a few leaves. Top middle sparse (near empty). Bottom middle entirely empty. Full alpha transparency through the center; no backgrounds, no paper fill, no architecture, no lettering, no gold geometric border. Warm late afternoon light, subdued color, the birds and florals remain contained within the corners, designed to frame a centered ornate sandstone temple without covering it.

## Mountains — public/art/mountains.webp

> Background ONLY for an original heritage Indian wedding invitation website, wide landscape 16:9 or wider. A light and airy vintage Indian miniature watercolor/gouache landscape on delicate ivory paper. Soft luminous pale warm ivory sunrise sky occupying the top 56%, very calm low-contrast open center and near top for readable dark wedding lettering laid over later (do not render any text). Misty layered distant mountains low across the lower half, more visible toward the far left and right, their hazy forms in pale dusty rose, muted lavender beige, and warm pale olive, fading to cream toward the middle and foreground. Golden morning light from center and wisps of cream mist. Subtle painterly shading, mist and paper grain, detailed fine-art atmosphere, warm muted pastel palette. The landscape should complement separate foreground artwork of a richly carved golden sandstone temple, painted dark olive leaves and antique brass bells. No temple, no structures, no foliage, no people, no animals, no text, no ornate borders, no dark colors, no large sun disk.

Image 2 is the existing illustration and is a STYLE and clothing palette reference only, never use those generic faces. Preserve the exact recognizable facial characteristics of the real woman and man from photo 1, the woman's open smile and his subtle smile, his swept-up dark hair and trimmed beard, her dark eyes, eyebrows and facial proportions. Keep bride on viewer's LEFT and taller groom on viewer's RIGHT as in photo, standing close together facing the viewer, with hands gently joined. Put the bride in deep burgundy Maharashtrian-inspired bridal lehenga and embroidered dupatta with understated antique gold jewelry. Put groom in warm ivory embroidered sherwani with burgundy stole, keep his swept dark hair visible without turban so he is immediately recognizable. Style: cute elegant storybook Indian miniature, detailed engraved linework and soft matte gouache, slightly softened proportions, warm gold highlights, same painted style as image 2. Square canvas with both subjects comfortably contained head to toe and roughly 7 percent transparent breathing room above heads and below shoes. Make faces sizable, detailed and legible at mobile sizes. A few small rose petals at their feet. Only the two subjects on true alpha transparent background; no scene, wall, frame, text, photo lighting, extra people or watermark.
